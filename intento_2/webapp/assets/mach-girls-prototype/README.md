# Mach-Girls prototype visual asset pack

This folder bundles real third-party game-art PNGs to replace the built-in procedural character and city placeholders during visual recovery.

- Four CC0 Warped City PNGs render as layered city scenery with parallax.
- Biker and Cyborg 48×48 sprite strips provide real idle, attack, hurt and defeat frames.
- The motorcycle sprite is loaded from its original CC0 URL (it is not bundled here); see `ASSET_CREDITS.md`.
- Explicit sources configured through Asset Studio override these fallbacks.
- These assets are prototype gameplay art. They do not represent approved final artwork or character canon.

See `ASSET_CREDITS.md` for author, source, license, and file-level provenance.
